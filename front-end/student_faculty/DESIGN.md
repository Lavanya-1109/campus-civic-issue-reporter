## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an authoritative, refined civic command aesthetic pairing architectural structure with crisp institutional micro-typography. Built for high-stakes campus infrastructure oversight and student-body issue reporting, the platform bridges operational rigor with accessible public service.

The design philosophy combines:
- **Architectural Structure:** Structural grids, disciplined panel divisions, and hairline demarcations inspired by civil blueprints and administrative registries.
- **Institutional Clarity:** Unambiguous information hierarchies where status, urgency, and departmental ownership can be parsed instantaneously.
- **Modern Civic Utility:** Clean white surfaces contrasted against deep navy and slate structures, eschewing frivolous ornamentation in favor of precision, high contrast, and tactile state feedback.

## Colors

The palette establishes an executive, institutional ground through deep slate foundations complemented by precise semantic accents for infrastructure states.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#1E293B`):** Deep Slate. Anchors top-level chrome, navigation hubs, prominent structural frames, and primary command buttons.
- **Secondary (`#3B82F6`):** Blueprint Blue. Handles interactive affordances, active selection states, focus highlights, and link triggers.
- **Tertiary (`#6366F1`):** Triage Indigo. Reserved for administrative dispatch, assignment pipelines, and operational queue routing.
- **Neutral Deep (`#0F172A`):** Obsidian Navy. Reserved for maximum contrast typography, high-priority headings, and dense data labels.

### Surface Architecture
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) establishes a quiet, glare-free working area for long reporting sessions.
- **Card & Sheet Surface:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) provides crisp elevated layers for tickets, inspection logs, and metric panels.
- **Subtle Partition:** `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) serves as hairline architectural borders to divide workspaces without heavy shadows.

### Semantic Status Indicators
- **Critical Urgency / Safety Hazard:** `#EF4444` (Rose 500) paired with `#FEF2F2` (Rose 50) for immediate structural warnings and emergency notices.
- **In Progress / Pending Review:** `#F59E0B` (Amber 500) paired with `#FFFBEB` (Amber 50) for operational tickets awaiting procurement or active work.
- **Resolved / Verified Clear:** `#10B981` (Emerald 500) paired with `#ECFDF5` (Emerald 50) for verified maintenance resolutions and safety sign-offs.
- **Assigned / Triage:** `#6366F1` (Indigo 500) paired with `#EEF2FF` (Indigo 50) for team routing and work-order scheduling.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances structural presence with extreme legibility under high information density. 

- **Headlines (`Manrope`):** Delivers clean geometry with engineered warmth. Used for incident headings, facility locations, and top-level analytics metrics.
- **Body & Micro-Copy (`Public Sans`):** Selected for its institutional clarity, neutral horizontal proportions, and exceptional legibility across dense data tables, activity logs, and technical maintenance workorders.
- **Micro-Labels & Metadata:** All status tags, ticket reference IDs, and role markers employ uppercase tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.04em`) via `label-sm` or `code-sm` to maintain instant visual separation from narrative descriptions.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a rigorous 8pt spatial rhythm (with 4pt half-steps for micro-components) designed to manage compact operational dashboards and expansive public incident maps.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (≥1280px):** 12-column grid with `layout-margin-desktop` (32px) and `layout-gutter-lg` (24px). Accommodates dual-pane triage interfaces: a fixed 380px audit list on the left with an expansive resolution pane on the right.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with `layout-margin-tablet` (24px) and `layout-gutter-md` (16px). Secondary telemetry panels collapse into tabbed side-drawers.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with `layout-margin-mobile` (16px) and `layout-gutter-sm` (12px). Full-width cards, pinned bottom-sheet action triggers, and single-column issue-intake sequences.

### Density Strategy
- **Administrative Consoles:** Utilize compact padding (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) inside tables and feeds to display high volume without scrolling fatigue.
- **Public Reporting Sequences:** Utilize expansive vertical spacing (`space-base` to `space-xl`) to reduce cognitive friction for students and faculty reporting immediate hazards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through structural planes, crisp hairline borders, and subtle tinted ambient occlusion rather than heavy drop shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F8FAFC`. The foundational canvas backdrop. Flat, inert.
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Data Rows):** `#FFFFFF` surface with a 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border and an ambient shadow: `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Records & Floating Controls):** `#FFFFFF` surface with a 1px solid `#CBD5E1` border and an elevated ambient shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Command Modals & Triage Drawers):** `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a 1px `#CBD5E1` border and deep directional shadow: `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 4px 8px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.
- **Overlay Shield:** Background scrim utilizes `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.45)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(2px)` to maintain context while focusing immediate attention on urgent tasks.

## Shapes

The design uses a Soft (`1`) curvature standard. Sharp, architectonic elements are softened with a disciplined 0.25rem (4px) baseline radius. This communicates institutional precision and structural permanence, avoiding the informal playfulness of large border radiuses.

### Geometry Hierarchy
- **Base Components (`rounded` - 4px):** Inputs, buttons, data-table cells, structural tabs, and compact status tags.
- **Structural Modules (`rounded-lg` - 8px):** Facility issue cards, analytic containers, dialog windows, and flyout navigation menus.
- **Surfacing Elements (`rounded-xl` - 12px):** Top-level workspace panels and multi-step reporting wrappers.
- **Special Status Badges & Avatars:** Fully rounded pill-shapes are reserved exclusively for circular indicators, status dot wrappers, and user presence markers to contrast against the prevailing rectangular grid.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#1E293B` surface, `#FFFFFF` text, `rounded` (4px). Hover state transitions to `#0F172A`. Active state scales subtly (`scale: 0.99`). Focus ring uses 2px offset with `#3B82F6`.
- **Secondary / Tactical:** Transparent surface, 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, text in `#1E293B`. Hover shifts background to `#F1F5F9`.
- **Urgent Action (Dispatch / Critical):** `#EF4444` background with white text, used strictly for reporting hazardous conditions or halting facilities.
- **Density Specs:** Default height 36px with 12px horizontal padding for administration; 44px with 16px horizontal padding for mobile student interactions.

### Badges & Chips
- **Structural Status Badges:** Compact pills with an inset 6px indicator dot.
  - *Critical:* `#FEF2F2` background, `#991B1B` text, `#EF4444` dot.
  - *In Progress:* `#FFFBEB` background, `#92400E` text, `#F59E0B` dot.
  - *Resolved:* `#ECFDF5` background, `#065F46` text, `#10B981` dot.
  - *Assigned / Triage:* `#EEF2FF` background, `#3730A3` text, `#6366F1` dot.
- **Role Indicator Badges:**
  - *Student / Faculty:* Neutral border `#E2E8F0`, background `#F8FAFC`, text `#475569`, 4px radius.
  - *Department Admin / Facility Dispatch:* Inverted contrast `#1E293B` background, `#F8FAFC` text, with bold uppercase typography to signal elevated permissions.

### Form Inputs & Select Fields
- **Container:** Pure `#FFFFFF` background, 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, 4px radius, height 38px.
- **Text:** Public Sans 14px (`body-md`), `#0F172A` value text, `#94A3B8` placeholder.
- **Interactive States:** Hover shifts border to `#94A3B8`. Focus delivers a crisp 1px solid `#3B82F6` boundary with an ambient `0 0 0 3px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.15)` focus ring.
- **Form Labels:** `label-md` in `#334155` positioned 6px above the field, paired with optional monospace requirement indicators (`[REQUIRED]`).

### Cards & Workorder Feed Panels
- **Anatomy:** `#FFFFFF` ground, 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, 8px radius.
- **Header:** Integrated hairline bottom border separating metadata (Ticket ID, Timestamp, Campus Sector) from description copy.
- **Interaction:** Hover triggers a border transition to `#94A3B8` and elevation Level 2.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes:** 16x16px squares with 3px radius. Unchecked state uses `#FFFFFF` with 1.5px solid `#CBD5E1`. Checked state fills with `#1E293B` displaying a crisp white micro-check.
- **Radio Buttons:** 16x16px circular controls with a 5px solid `#1E293B` center point when selected.

### Critical Safety Banners
- Dedicated notification surface at the top of feeds for active campus-wide alerts (e.g., steam pipe rupture, electrical outage). Features a 4px solid left border in `#EF4444`, background `#FEF2F2`, `#7F1D1D` text, and immediate action buttons for dispatch logs.
